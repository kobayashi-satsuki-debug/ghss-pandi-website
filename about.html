About G H S S Pandi

A government higher secondary school in Kasaragod district, serving generations of families from Pandi and nearby villages.

Since 1929

A public school with a long memory

Government Higher Secondary School Pandi began as a village school and grew into a full higher secondary school serving Classes 1 to 12.

It was founded in 1929 and has served Pandi for nearly a hundred years.

The school follows the Kerala State syllabus and is part of the government school system.

At a glance

  • Classes 1 to 10 and Plus One / Plus Two Commerce
  • Commerce stream at Plus One and Plus Two
  • Science labs, library, computer room and playground
  • JRC unit (Junior Red Cross), arts and sports clubs
  • Office hours 9:30 am – 3:30 pm
