About the school

A public school with a long memory and a forward look

Established in 1948, GHSS Pandi has been the neighbourhood school of generations of families in Pandi and nearby villages of Kasaragod district, offering Classes 1 to 10 and Commerce at higher secondary level.

  • Classes 1 to 10, then Plus One and Plus Two Commerce
  • Science laboratories, computer lab and library
  • NSS, Scouts, arts club, sports and eco club
  • Bilingual learning support in Malayalam and English
